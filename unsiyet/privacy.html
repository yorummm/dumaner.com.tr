Gizlilik Politikası

Son güncelleme: 24 Ekim 2026

Ünsiyet; namaz vakitleri, kıble pusulası, Kur'an-ı Kerim, hadis, dua ve zikirmatiği bir araya getiren, yapay zekâ destekli bir İslami yaşam asistanıdır. Dini metinler yalnızca güvenilir yayınlardan alınır ve değiştirilmeden gösterilir.

Cihazında saklananlar

Favorilerin, okuma geçmişin, son okuduğun ayet, zikir sayaçların, ayarların, sohbet geçmişin ve son konumun yalnızca bu cihazda saklanır. Ünsiyet'in bir üyelik sistemi yoktur; sunucularımızda senin hakkında kişisel veri tutulmaz.

Konum

Konumun yalnızca namaz vakitlerini ve kıble yönünü hesaplamak için cihazında kullanılır, sunucularımıza gönderilmez. Bulunduğun ilçenin adını göstermek için koordinatların cihazının konum servisine (Apple veya Google) iletilir.

Sohbet

Sohbette yazdıkların ve seçtiğin ruh hâli, cevap üretilmesi için sunucumuz üzerinden yapay zekâ sağlayıcısına iletilir. Sunucumuz mesajlarını kaydetmez. Sağlayıcının verileri nasıl işlediği kendi gizlilik politikasına tabidir.

Ücretsiz soru hakkı

Ücretsiz soruları saymak için uygulama, sohbet isteklerine rastgele bir kurulum kodu ekler. Sunucumuz yalnızca bu kodun soru sayısını ve kötüye kullanımı önlemek için IP adresinin geri çevrilemez bir özetiyle günlük istek sayısını kısa süre tutar; bunlar adına veya bir hesaba bağlanmaz. Premium, satın alma kanıtı Apple veya Google'a sorularak doğrulanır.

Bildirimler

Namaz vakti hatırlatmaları cihazında planlanır; bu bilgi hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez.

Satın almalar

Premium satın alımları App Store veya Google Play üzerinden yapılır. Ödeme bilgilerin bize ulaşmaz.

Verilerinin kontrolü

Profil → Ayarlar → Veri bölümünden verilerini dışa aktarabilir veya tamamını silebilirsin.

Yapay zekâ sağlayıcıları

Ünsiyet sohbeti OpenAI'nin dil modelleriyle çalışır. Mesajların, cevap üretilmesi için OpenAI API'sine iletilir. Mikrofonla yazdığında ses kaydın yalnızca yazıya çevrilmesi için OpenAI'ye gönderilir, uygulamada saklanmaz. Model ayet, hadis veya dua metni yazmaz; önerilen her metin uygulamanın kendi doğrulanmış kaynaklarından gösterilir.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ünsiyet is an AI-assisted Islamic life companion that brings together prayer times, a qibla compass, the Holy Quran, hadiths, duas and a dhikr counter. Religious texts come only from trusted publications and are shown unchanged.

Kept on your device

Your favorites, reading history, last read verse, dhikr counts, settings, chat history and last location are kept only on this device. Ünsiyet has no accounts; we keep no personal data about you on our servers.

Location

Your location is used on your device only to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction, and is not sent to our servers. To show the name of your area, the coordinates are passed to your device’s location service (Apple or Google).

Chat

What you write in the chat and the mood you pick are passed through our server to the AI provider to produce a reply. Our server does not store your messages. How the provider handles data is subject to its own privacy policy.

Free questions

To count the free questions, the app adds a random install code to chat requests. Our server keeps only this code's question count and, to prevent abuse, a daily request count under a one-way hash of the IP address for a short time; neither is linked to your name or an account. Premium is confirmed by checking the purchase proof with Apple or Google.

Notifications

Prayer time reminders are scheduled on your device; this information is not sent to any server.

Purchases

Premium purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us.

Control of your data

You can export or delete all your data in Profile → Settings → Data.

AI providers

The Ünsiyet chat works with OpenAI’s language models. Your messages are passed to the OpenAI API to produce a reply. When you dictate, your voice recording is sent to OpenAI only to be converted to text and is not kept by the app. The model does not write verse, hadith or dua texts; every suggested text is shown from the app’s own verified sources.

Obrolan

Untuk menghitung pertanyaan gratis, aplikasi menambahkan kode instalasi acak pada permintaan obrolan. Server kami hanya menyimpan jumlah pertanyaan kode ini dan, untuk mencegah penyalahgunaan, jumlah permintaan harian dengan hash satu arah dari alamat IP untuk waktu singkat; keduanya tidak dikaitkan dengan nama atau akun Anda. Pengingat waktu salat dijadwalkan di perangkat Anda; informasi ini tidak dikirim ke server mana pun.

Pembelian

Pembelian Premium dilakukan melalui App Store atau Google Play. Detail pembayaran Anda tidak pernah sampai kepada kami.

Kendali atas data Anda

Penyedia AI

Saat Anda mendikte, rekaman suara Anda dikirim ke OpenAI hanya untuk diubah menjadi teks dan tidak disimpan oleh aplikasi. বিনামূল্যের প্রশ্ন গোনার জন্য অ্যাপটি কথোপকথনের অনুরোধে একটি এলোমেলো ইনস্টল কোড যোগ করে। আমাদের সার্ভার শুধু এই কোডের প্রশ্নসংখ্যা এবং অপব্যবহার ঠেকাতে IP ঠিকানার একমুখী হ্যাশসহ দৈনিক অনুরোধসংখ্যা অল্প সময়ের জন্য রাখে; এগুলোর কোনোটিই আপনার নাম বা কোনো অ্যাকাউন্টের সঙ্গে যুক্ত নয়। প্রিমিয়াম যাচাই করা হয় কেনাকাটার প্রমাণ Apple বা Google-এর কাছে পরীক্ষা করে।

বিজ্ঞপ্তি

নামাজের সময়ের রিমাইন্ডার আপনার ডিভাইসে নির্ধারিত হয়; এই তথ্য কোনো সার্ভারে পাঠানো হয় না।

কেনাকাটা

প্রিমিয়াম কেনাকাটা App Store বা Google Play-এর মাধ্যমে হয়। আপনার পেমেন্টের তথ্য কখনো আমাদের কাছে পৌঁছায় না।

আপনার ডেটার নিয়ন্ত্রণ

আপনি প্রোফাইল → সেটিংস → ডেটা থেকে আপনার সব ডেটা এক্সপোর্ট বা মুছতে পারেন।

এআই সরবরাহকারী

Ünsiyet-এর কথোপকথন OpenAI-এর ভাষা মডেল দিয়ে চলে। উত্তর তৈরির জন্য আপনার বার্তা OpenAI API-তে পাঠানো হয়। বলে লেখার সময় আপনার কণ্ঠের রেকর্ডিং শুধু লেখায় রূপান্তরের জন্য OpenAI-তে পাঠানো হয় এবং অ্যাপ তা রাখে না। মডেল আয়াত, হাদিস বা দোয়ার পাঠ লেখে না; প্রতিটি সুপারিশকৃত পাঠ অ্যাপের নিজস্ব যাচাইকৃত উৎস থেকে দেখানো হয়।
