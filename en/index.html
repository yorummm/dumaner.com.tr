Say what is on your heart. Ünsiyet listens.

The AI in the chat never writes verses or hadiths itself. It searches the app’s verified translations, hadith and dua collections and tells you why each one fits.

  1. Pick how you feel

    Peaceful, anxious, sad, tired… Tap one or write in your own words.

  2. Share what is on your mind

    Ünsiyet listens. If you would rather not type, tap the microphone and speak.

  3. It searches verified sources

    Quran translations, hadith and dua collections are searched. No made-up references.

  4. Suggestions that fit you

    Verses, hadiths, duas and dhikr, each with the reason it was chosen. Tap one to read it in full.

Where do the texts come from?

Religious texts come only from trusted publications and are shown unchanged.

  • The Holy Quran, Arabic textTanzil Project, Uthmani script, version 1.1
  • Translations and tafsirQuranEnc.com; English: Saheeh International; Al-Mukhtasar tafsir
  • HadithsHadeethEnc.com; with explanation and grading
  • RecitationQuran.Foundation; 12 reciters, streamed
  • Masnavi (Turkish)Türkiye Manuscripts Institution translation, with permission
  • Prayer timesCalculated on the device with your region’s method
